Privacy Policy – FatTiger Assistant (demo)
 Last updated: 9-Nov-2026

 What this is. FatTiger Assistant is a demonstration WhatsApp and web chatbot built by me that answers questions about menu items, prices and shop information for the fictional "FatTiger" shops.

 What we collect. When you message the assistant, we receive your phone number and the text of your message from WhatsApp (via Meta or Twilio).

 How we use it. Your message is used only to generate a reply. To do this, the message text is sent to our AI provider (OpenAI). Your phone number is used to decide which information you may see (for example, staff vs. customer).

 What we store. We keep a log of which tools the assistant used to answer (for example, "looked up product MM-P"), with your phone number partially masked (e.g. +918•••641), for security and debugging. We do not store a history of your conversations. Server logs may contain message content temporarily and are deleted automatically by our hosting provider.

 Sharing. We do not sell or share your data. It is processed only by the services needed to run the assistant: Meta (WhatsApp), Twilio, OpenAI and Render (hosting).

 Deleting your data. To request deletion of any data related to your phone number, email [m.ambikesh@gmail.com] with the subject "Delete my data". We will respond within 30 days.
